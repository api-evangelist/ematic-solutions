Ecommerce SEO: 7 Trends Dominating Online Stores in 2027 Many retail executives still operate under the dangerous assumption that optimizing an online store merely requires stuffing category pages and product descriptions with high-volume keywords. This outdated methodology actively harms revenue generation as search engine algorithms have evolved far beyond basic lexical matching. Modern algorithms operate on semantic, entity-based understanding, meaning they map the intricate relationships between products, brands, and user intent rather than just counting words on a page.
