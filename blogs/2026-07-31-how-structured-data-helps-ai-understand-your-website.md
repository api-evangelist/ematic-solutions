Artificial intelligence is transforming how people search for information online. Instead of simply matching keywords, AI-powered search engines now interpret context, relationships, and user intent to deliver more accurate answers. As AI search continues to evolve through platforms like Google’s AI Overview, ChatGPT, Perplexity and other AI assistants, websites need to provide information in a format that machines can easily understand.
