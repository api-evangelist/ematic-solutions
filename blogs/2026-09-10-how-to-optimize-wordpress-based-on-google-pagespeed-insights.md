A Google PageSpeed Insights (PSI) report can show that a page has a slow server response, poor Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) or excessive main-thread work. However, it cannot tell you which WordPress component is responsible or whether a suggested change is safe for the website. At Ematic Solutions, we treat the PSI report as the starting point to optimize WordPress, rather than the solution itself.
